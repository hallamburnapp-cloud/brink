# The market across phone, Steam and web

Gathered by web research in October 2026. Every claim carries its source and a confidence level.

## Findings

- **Premium (pay-once) is a small but growing part of mobile games. Free-to-play still takes about 96% of downloads.** (medium confidence)
  - Evidence: AppMagic data, as reported by GamesIndustry.biz and Pocket Tactics (updated 9 Jul 2026): premium was about 4% of mobile game downloads in 2025. Premium releases rose 77%, from 422 in 2024 to nearly 750 in 2025. Mobile revenue: Balatro $21.3m from 3.1m downloads at $9.99; Slay the Spire $13.7m; Dead Cells $6.5m.
  - Source: https://www.pockettactics.com/premium-mobile-games-increase
- **Mobile game revenue is flat overall. Strategy was the only mobile genre that grew, but that growth is free-to-play 4X with live operations, not premium.** (high confidence)
  - Evidence: Sensor Tower State of Gaming (26 Feb 2026): mobile in-app purchase revenue was $82B in 2025 (+1%). 4X Strategy was 'the only mobile genre to grow in revenue, downloads, and time spent'. The #1 and #2 mobile games by revenue were Last War: Survival and Whiteout Survival. Steam premium revenue was $12B.
  - Source: https://sensortower.com/press/press-release-sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-gaming-drove-52-billion-downloads-82b-iap-revenue-on-mobile-and-12b-premium-revenue-on-steam
- **Map strategy games are still among the best-selling paid iOS games today. Their prices cluster at £0.99–£4.99, with big-name ports at £6.99–£9.99.** (high confidence)
  - Evidence: Live iOS Top Paid Games chart, pulled 13 Oct 2026. UK: #2 Plague Inc. £0.99, #8 Bloons TD 6 £6.99, #12 Balatro £9.99, #13 Rebel Inc. £1.99, #14 Kingdom Rush 6 £6.99, #15 No. 10: Full Confidence £2.99, #17 After Inc. £1.99, #22 Mini Metro £3.99, #24 Slay the Spire £9.99, #31 Papers, Please £4.99. US: #5 Plague Inc. $0.99, #25 Rebel Inc. $1.99, #26 After Inc. $1.99, #50 Mini Metro $3.99. Other UK prices: Northgard £2.99, Reigns £2.99, Bad North £3.99, Townscaper £4.99.
  - Source: https://itunes.apple.com/gb/rss/toppaidapplications/limit=50/genre=6014/json ; https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/toppaidapplications/limit=50/genre=6014/json ; https://itunes.apple.com/search (lookups, 13 Oct 2026)
- **Ndemic, the closest commercial precedent, sells paid upfront on iOS and free with in-app purchases on Android. The game with the widest reach (Plague Inc.) also shows ads on Android. Rebel Inc. and After Inc. show no ads label.** (high confidence)
  - Evidence: Google Play UK listings, fetched 13 Oct 2026. Plague Inc.: 'Contains ads', 'In-app purchases', 4.7 stars, 4.06m reviews, 100m+ downloads, PEGI 7.   - Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.miniclip.plagueinc ; https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ndemiccreations.rebelinc ; https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ndemiccreations.afterinc ; https://www.androidpolice.com/2019/02/11/rebel-inc-is-the-spiritual-successor-to-plague-inc-and-its-officially-available-on-android/

  - Evidence: Pocket Gamer (24 May 2022): Plague Inc. passed 180 million downloads. Founder James Vaughan: 'I'd never made a video game before, Plague Inc. was just a hobby.' The After Inc. App Store description (2026) now claims 'over 190 million players'. It also says After Inc. 'is not based on any real world' event.

  - Source: https://www.gamesbrief.com/2010/09/hungry-shark-goes-free-leaps-to-1-free-app-3-paid-app-in-the-us/ ; https://www.pocketgamer.biz/hosting-26-million-trials-during-q1-2012-flexion-sees-conversion-rates-on-its-android-game-wrapper-of-over-10/ ; https://alpha.developers.meta.com/horizon/blog/pistol-whip-75-conversion-rate-try-before-you-buy-tbyb-insights-best-practices
- **On the App Store, the decision to install is made in seconds from the icon, the first one to three screenshots and the first seconds of the video.** (medium confidence)
  - Evidence: Apple: up to 10 screenshots and up to 3 app previews of 30s each. Previews 'autoplay with muted audio… make sure the first few seconds… are visually compelling'. 'The first one to three images will appear in search results when no app preview is available' (three portrait or one landscape). Product page A/B tests and custom product pages are free. AppAgent/Phiture (2017, small A/B sample): visitors spend about 7 seconds on a store page. 13% scroll the screenshots (9% portrait, 5% landscape, 17% for games); about 2% play the video; under 1% expand the description.

  - Evidence: AppTweak 2025 (US), page views to installs: App Store 8.56% average, Google Play 16.15%. GameAnalytics 2026 benchmarks (2025 data, 16,262 titles, all genres): top-quartile D1 retention just above 30%, top 10% at 40%, median D7 just under 4%.

## Implications for our design
- Treat phones as the main market and Steam as the second. The comparable map games have about 60x more Google Play reviews than Steam reviews (Plague 4.06m vs 66k; Polytopia 237k vs 6k). Design touch-first, with the whole board readable on a phone. The Steam/PC version is the same game with mouse and keyboard and a bigger canvas, and must play well on Steam Deck.
- Use a fully invented world (new continents and coastlines), not an Earth-shaped map with names removed. Store rules require it (Apple 1.1.2 on real governments as enemies). A real-world map coloured by a spreading tint is also Plague Inc.'s signature look. Ndemic's three games are all in today's UK top 20 paid, so reviewers and players will spot a lookalike at once. Also avoid any 'X Inc.' name, a disease, DNA-like bubble pickups and a left-hand upgrade tree like Ndemic's.
- Move the premise away from disease and from a literal nuclear war between great powers, or at least do not sell the game on them. Disease meets Apple 1.1.7, Google's Sensitive Events policy, the China removal precedent and the clone problem. Real-looking war meets TikTok's war-imagery ban, Meta's foreign-policy ad checks, and Poki's and CrazyGames' age rules. Themes the market currently rewards and that pass all stores: rebuilding or restoring a world (After Inc., Terra Nil), a god or network view of a living map (WorldBox, Mini Metro), or a constructive force spreading against a non-human counter-force. Tension and stakes can stay, but in a fictional, non-graphic register that rates around 9+/12+ and PEGI 7–12.
- Mobile money: free to download, with one permanent 'Full game' unlock and no ads, no currencies and no consumables. On Android this copies Rebel Inc., After Inc. and Polytopia. The no-tracking rule leaves only low-value 'limited ads', so ads are not worth it. Price the unlock at £3.99/$3.99, inside the £2.99–£4.99 band where Mini Metro, Northgard, Townscaper and Papers, Please sit. The first world or scenario must be free and finishable to a clear win or loss before the purchase screen.
