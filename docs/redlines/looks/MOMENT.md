# The moment every look must draw (identical across the three directions)

Portrait phone frame, exactly 390 x 844 CSS px. BRINK is the working title (fictional world, fictional powers,
no real flags, insignia, brands or people). The game: the Federation (the Cap, capital Wintermast, north) is
pushing; you lead the Republic (Highmarch, capital Edda Vale, west). You make public promises to defend places
(WE DEFEND); when a promised place is pushed, that is a test and the phone rings: BACK IT / BACK DOWN / WAIT.
Two things race: WAR (regions fighting; 8 of 30 is world war) and TREATY (the Federation's willingness to sign;
100 is the treaty). One word says how far up the ladder they are: TESTING, PUSHING, MASSING, FIGHTING, ALL-OUT.

## 03:14 (the call frame)
The same world, the clock stopped, the phone ringing. Caller: General Ruun Halder (the Hawk). Message, 40 words
or fewer, for example: "Federation armour is at Lintel, the promise you inherited. Two brigades, lights on, not
moving. They want to see what you do." Then advisers in character, one line each, with NO conditional
annotations about what kind of enemy this is (never "if they are scared of us..."):
- Hawk General: "Move now. Or never promise anything again."
- Dove Foreign Minister: "A brigade in the Sound is the thing they warned us about."
- Intel Director (observations only): "Fourth place they've pushed. First promise they've tested."
Choices, each with its cost in plain words:
- BACK IT: move the brigade up · costs 12 influence · we keep our word
- BACK DOWN: protest and hold · they'll believe us less · Lintel will likely fall
- WAIT a day · Intel: Lintel may fall · a second source may arrive

## The test of every screen
Someone who never plays games, watching over the player's shoulder, can say what is happening and what the
player is trying to do: "the Federation, up top, keeps pushing; she promised to defend places and now they're
testing one; the blue bar is them agreeing to stop."
